## 8. Referral & gate tracker — tier roles tied to the flywheel

Recognition ladder in the discussion group, awarded manually by you (I draft
the shoutouts). Tiers key off *contribution to the flywheel*, never off
invite counts alone (invite-farming = fake members = dead group).

| Tier | How it's earned | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| **Runner** | joins + introduces themselves in #introductions | post in discussion group |
| **Scout** | brings 1 member who actually engages (3+ real messages) | name in Friday post shoutout |
| **Operator** | runs a verifiable flywheel action: trades on the AltQuick books we track, sends a DOGE/CLAM deposit, or contributes liquidity-relevant info that checks out | "Operator" role + early access to weekly treasury full logs |
| **Fixer** | any contribution that measurably moves the system (a referral who deposits, a partner intro, a bug found in the site/desk) | "Fixer" role + Syndicate Voice Rank on smc.kimi.page (same gamification layer the site already has) |
| **Canon** | sustained contribution over a month (Ops/Fixer actions weekly) | named in the site's TUNNEL canon page as a Syndicate operator — the lore tier |

Rules that keep this honest:
- Every tier claim must point at something verifiable (a txid, a message, a
  merged fix). "Trust me" never counts — same receipts-only culture as the desk.
- Roles are recognition, not financial promises. No tier ever implies returns,
  payouts, or insider signals. State that in the pinned rules.
- Track awards in the loop ledger (`type:"tier_award"`) so the weekly report
  can celebrate new Operators/Fixers — promotion content for free.
